Privacy Policy

Sown by Morning · last updated 25 November 2026

Sown by Morning is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the stars you earned on each stretch, the daily stretches you finished, and how many helpers (Glimpse, Reverse, Rain) you hold.
  • Your settings: whether sound is on.
  • Whether you have bought the Viaduct Line, the Porter's Trolley or the Station Patron, and the store's transaction ids, so a purchase is never given twice.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.
  • The game makes no network requests of its own; it talks only to the App Store, for purchases.

Purchases

The 60 stretches of the free line and the daily stretch are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
