Sown by Morning

Sow a few seeds. Wake to a garden.

A calm puzzle game on an old railway line: plant a few seeds, say goodnight, and see how each wild plant spreads while you sleep.

Why play

Every plant has its own way

Strawberries run into the next bed, touch-me-not pods fling a seed two beds away, and ants carry violets to their nest. Learn the habits, then plan around them.

A few minutes a day

60 free stretches across four stations, and a new daily stretch every morning. There is no timer and no energy to wait for.

Quiet by design

No ads, no account, no tracking. It plays offline, and nothing you do leaves your phone.

How a night works

  1. 1 · Sow and waterChoose where your few seeds go, and spend the day's pours.
  2. 2 · Say goodnightOvernight, watered seeds sprout and blooming plants spread.
  3. 3 · Read the morningSee what grew, then fill every stake before the season turns.
