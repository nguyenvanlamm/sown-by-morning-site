How to play

Each stretch of the old line is a puzzle. During the day, sow a few seeds and water them with the day's pours, then tap Goodnight. Overnight, watered seeds sprout and each blooming plant spreads in its own way. Fill every stake before the season turns; finish by the par night for three stars. A tagged stake only counts with the plant on its tag. Tap the tin in your hand to read its plant's habit again.

Helpers

Glimpse shows tonight before you say goodnight, Reverse takes you back one morning, and Rain wakes every sleeping seed. You earn one the first time you finish a stretch with three stars, and one for each daily stretch.

Purchases

The free line (60 stretches) and the daily stretch are free. In the Booking office you can buy the Viaduct Line (24 more stretches with bramble and mint), the Porter's Trolley (5 Glimpse, 5 Reverse and 5 Rain) or the Station Patron (a copper can, amber lamps and a blackbird; no gameplay). Bought something before, or on another device with the same store account? Open Settings → Restore purchases, or the Restore line in the Booking office.

Progress

Progress is saved on the device only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
